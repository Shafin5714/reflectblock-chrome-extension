# ReflectBlock: Website & Adult Site Blocker

## Store summary

Free website and adult-content blocker with Shorts/Reels controls, focus schedules, and local-only settings.

## Full description

All features are completely free. No ads, no account, and no subscription. Your settings stay on your device.

ReflectBlock helps you build a calmer browser by blocking distractions and adult-content sites on your terms.

- Block entire websites, specific URL paths, or a keyword appearing in a website URL.
- Block YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and Instagram Reels while keeping normal content available.
- Use focus schedules to apply your website and keyword rules during selected hours.
- Use Adult Content Shield, powered by a bundled local domain list.
- Turn on Strict Mode to add a 15-minute intentional delay before protection can be disabled.
- Optionally open a personal reminder page or family-photo URL after removal.

ReflectBlock does not require an account. Your blocking rules and settings are stored locally in Chrome, not sent to ReflectBlock or sold to advertisers.
